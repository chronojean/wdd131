W05 Project — Website Planning Document

Benjamin Academy

A real-life academy website for English courses (A1–B2) and office/computer skills training in Excel, Word, PowerPoint, data analysis, and public speaking.

1. Site Name

Benjamin Academy is the chosen site name because it is the actual name of the academy and gives the website a trustworthy, professional identity that matches the real business.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the site is to present Benjamin Academy as a place where learners can discover English learning options and practical office skills courses. The site will provide course information, schedule ideas, enrollment guidance, and references for users who want to study with structure and confidence.

3. Scenario Questions

  • What English levels are available, and what topics are taught in each level?
  • Which office skills modules are offered, and what practical outcomes can students expect?
  • How can a visitor contact the academy to request information or enroll in a course?

4. Color Scheme

#0B3D91 Primary navy for headings, navigation, and key actions.
#1BA3A6 Accent teal for buttons, highlights, and sections.
#F4E7D8 Soft sand background for contrast and readability.
#1F2933 Dark text for body content and strong visual contrast.

5. Typography

Heading font: Montserrat. Used for page titles, section titles, and key labels.

Body font: Open Sans. Used for paragraphs, lists, and general content readability.
