01 — Identity

Site Name & Brand

Site Name: Benjamin Academy

Benjamin Academy is an educational platform built around two complementary pillars of professional growth:

  • Conversational English Mastery — Practical, scenario-driven fluency training across CEFR levels A1, A2, B1, and B2. Every lesson centers on real-world communication: job interviews, team meetings, client calls, and social interaction.
  • Ofimatic & Executive Business Skills — Hands-on modules covering computer informatics fundamentals, Microsoft Excel & data analysis, professional Word report writing, business PowerPoint & data-driven presentation design, and executive speech & communication skills.
02 — Purpose

Site Purpose & Audience

The web application gives prospective students, working professionals, and job seekers a dynamic, interactive platform to explore course offerings, evaluate their current skill level through an automated quiz, build customized learning packages, and enroll in classes — all from a single responsive interface.

Target Audience

  • Young Professionals & Job Seekers — Gaining conversational English confidence (B1-B2) for international job interviews and remote career opportunities.
  • Administrative & Corporate Staff — Mastering Excel data analysis, structured Word reports, and executive PowerPoint slideshow presentations.
  • Beginner & Intermediate Students — Building foundational digital informatics skills alongside step-by-step English instruction from A1 upward.

Audience Scenarios

Scenario 01

English Fluency Evaluation

“I know some basic English, but I’m not sure if I’m A2 or B1. How can I find out which class is right for me?”

Solution: An interactive Level Assessment Quiz on the Subscribe page evaluates conversational skills and automatically recommends the matching course track.

Scenario 02

Custom Skill Combination

“I need both Excel Data Analysis and B1 Conversational English. Can I combine both into a single weekly schedule?”

Solution: A Custom Package Builder widget lets users select combined English + Ofimatic modules with real-time total hours and pricing calculations.

Scenario 03

Course Structure & Enrollment

“What specific topics are covered in the Ofimatic course, and how do I sign up for a trial class?”

Solution: The Ofimatic page features interactive detail drawers for each module, plus a streamlined registration form that persists state via localStorage.

05 — Architecture

Site Architecture

Benjamin Academy comprises five interconnected pages:

Landing Page
index.html

Hero banner, academy overview, featured English & Ofimatic modules, quiz teaser, package builder overview, testimonials, and footer.

English Courses
english.html

Detailed CEFR level breakdowns for A1, A2, B1, and B2 with conversational practice structure and interactive level selector.

Ofimatic & Business
ofimatic.html

Informatics, Excel data analysis, Word report writing, business PPT design, and executive speech presentation modules.

Subscribe & Quiz
subscribe.html

Interactive 4-step placement quiz, custom package builder, validated registration form, and localStorage persistence.

Thank You
thankyou.html

Personalized confirmation receipt reading saved registration details from localStorage and displaying enrollment summary.
